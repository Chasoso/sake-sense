# Product constraints

These are the current stable-enough constraints for experiments. They are distinct from the provisional hypotheses in `docs/hypotheses/` and from implementation details.

## Stable constraints

- The project must preserve a meaningful connection to Ishikawa sake or sake culture.
- A demo must be possible without serving alcohol, and participation must not require drinking alcohol.
- Local UX validation must work without AWS or other external infrastructure.
- Core functionality must not require a comprehensive database of all Ishikawa sake brands.
- Product-specific data, when needed, should begin with a small, well-sourced set.
- Publicly visible web information must not automatically be treated as open or reusable data.
- Nonverbal expression may be an entrance toward language; it is not a reason to reject language or professional sake terminology.
- No experimental mapping is authoritative or scientifically validated merely because it appears in a prototype.

## Human-owned decisions

Humans own product vision, UX judgment, hypothesis acceptance or rejection, major architecture decisions, and the final keep / revise / reject decision for experiments. Codex may implement an explicit Issue but must stop when a requested change would materially alter these decisions without authorization.

## Not constraints yet

The following remain open hypotheses or future decisions: which modality is most useful, whether a shared sensory representation improves translation, the contents of a sensory dictionary, whether feedback should train a future system, the final product flow, the authoritative data sources, and AWS or AI architecture.
