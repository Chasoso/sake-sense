# H010 - AI-assisted semantic interpretation improves translation fidelity without sacrificing transparency

- **Status:** unvalidated
- **Owner of decision:** Human

## Working statement

A constrained AI bridge may interpret combinations of observable body-motion features more naturally than fixed one-feature-to-one-term mappings while preserving trust when observable motion remains deterministic, sake terms remain closed-set, zero candidates are valid, reasoning is visible, and unsupported features remain unmapped.

## Boundary

This is an experiment about translating intentional expression into possible sensory language. It is not a claim that AI can detect taste, preference, emotion, personality, health, intoxication, or the best sake. Human review owns the decision to keep, revise, or reject this hypothesis.
