# H007 - Whole-body expression improves engagement

- **Status:** unvalidated
- **Owner of decision:** Human
- **Related experiment:** [EXP-003 - Whole-body movement tasting interface](../experiments/EXP-003-body-expression.md)

## Hypothesis

A short whole-body movement may feel more natural, memorable, and repeatable for expressing a sensation than keyboard, voice, or finger-only input.

This is an experiment hypothesis, not a claim that body movement measures taste or emotion.

## Human review

Compare EXP-003 with EXP-002 for comfort, clarity, effort, playfulness, and willingness to try again. Review whether the movement description feels like a reflection of the user's action rather than a judgment about the user.
