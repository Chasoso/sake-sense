## Status vocabulary

- `unvalidated`: an active belief without sufficient experiment evidence.
- `keep`: human review found it validated enough to continue.
- `revise`: evidence changes the wording or scope of the hypothesis.
- `reject`: evidence is sufficient to stop pursuing the hypothesis in its current form.

Only a human decides the final status. Failed ideas remain in this history; they are not silently removed.
