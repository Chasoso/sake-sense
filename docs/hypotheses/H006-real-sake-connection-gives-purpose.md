# H006 — Real sake connection gives the translation a purpose

- **Status:** unvalidated
- **Owner of decision:** Human
- **Related experiment:** [EXP-002 — Voice and richer expression](../experiments/EXP-002-voice-richer-expression.md)

## Hypothesis

A translation from sensation to sake-related language may feel incomplete if it ends at terminology alone. Connecting the resulting term to real, source-grounded Ishikawa sake may help the user understand why the translation matters and create curiosity about trying or learning about the sake.

This connection is an exploration/example, not an authoritative recommendation or compatibility diagnosis.

## Rationale and assumptions

EXP-001 human observations reported that the next connection after a sake term was unclear and identified real sake as a promising direction. A product connection may provide purpose, but it may also feel arbitrary or be mistaken for personalized advice if provenance and framing are weak.

## Experiment idea

For a small set of source-grounded Ishikawa sake products, show how a displayed sake term also appears in the product's sourced description. Explain the connection and its provenance without ranking products or claiming that Sake Sense independently evaluated their taste.

## Success signals

- The user understands why a specific Ishikawa sake is shown after the translated term.
- The connection reduces the feeling that the experience stops at terminology.
- The user becomes curious about tasting or learning more about the sake.
- The result is understood as an exploration/example rather than a recommendation.

## Failure signals

- The sake introduction feels arbitrary or bolted on.
- The user interprets the result as “this sake is objectively right for me.”
- Product data or provenance is too weak to explain why the sake was shown.
- The additional step adds information but not motivation.

## Evidence and follow-up

No EXP-002 human result has been recorded. The human decides `keep`, `revise`, or `reject` after reviewing the product sample and experience.
