# H008 - Body-to-sake bridge has purpose

- **Status:** unvalidated
- **Owner of decision:** Human
- **Related experiment:** [EXP-003 - Whole-body movement tasting interface](../experiments/EXP-003-body-expression.md)

## Hypothesis

Showing observable body movement features, experimental sensory hints, grounded sake language, and source-backed Ishikawa sake examples in one trail may make the translation feel understandable and purposeful.

The product step is an example for exploration, not a recommendation, ranking, diagnosis, or claim about the user's preferences.

## Human review

Check whether the feature-to-term and term-to-product connections are understandable, whether the provenance is sufficiently visible, and whether the experience creates curiosity without overclaiming.
